Questions

Good to know before you book

Can't find your answer? Ask us in the quote form.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

No. Many clients give us a key or door code. If you prefer to be home, that works too.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. We bring plant-based products and our own equipment. If you want us to use specific products, just tell us.

How is pricing worked out?

We price by home size, condition and service type after a short quote request. Quotes are free and there is no obligation.

Can you clean around pets?

Yes. We use pet-friendly products and will follow your instructions for keeping pets comfortable.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

Let us know within 24 hours and we will return to correct the issue at no charge.

Do you offer recurring service?

Yes: weekly, every two weeks, or monthly. One-time and deep cleans are available too.
