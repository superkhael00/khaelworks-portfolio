AI Candidate Screening & Shortlisting System

A deployable n8n template for BPOs, recruiters and staffing agencies. It reads every PDF resume, scores it against the client's own rubric with quoted evidence, and hands the recruiter a ranked list. The recruiter makes every decision; the system sends the emails, the questionnaire and the interview booking that follow.

The problem

Screening by hand is slow, uneven and hard to defend

Before

  • Hundreds of resumes per opening, each opened, read and logged by hand
  • Two recruiters score the same resume differently, with no record of why
  • Candidates wait days for any reply; good ones accept other offers
  • Decision emails, questionnaires and interview times are copy-paste work
  • Tools that auto-reject people cannot explain their reasons

After (v1.0)

  • Every resume scored against one rubric, each score backed by a quote
  • An acknowledgment reaches the candidate in about 20 seconds
  • The recruiter picks Advance, Hold or Reject from a dropdown; the email follows
  • Strong candidates get a secure questionnaire link and book their own interview
  • An Audit Log records every score, email and decision with its reason
How it works

One workflow, five lanes, two human decisions

Amber boxes are the only places a candidate moves forward or out. The client changes the job, rubric, questions, emails and timing in the Sheet, never inside n8n.

AI logic

The AI reads, code checks, a person decides

AIScores with evidence

Scores each rubric criterion and Stage 2 answer 0–5 against written anchors, quoting the line behind every score. Labels each claim in the questionnaire against the resume.

CodeChecks and calculates

Confirms each quote really appears, does every total and band, flags only unsupported or inflated claims, and stops the run on invalid AI output instead of saving a wrong score.

RecruiterMakes every decision

Advance, Hold, Reject and Schedule Interview are set by a person. Emails wait out an undo window. Knockouts are the candidate's own Yes/No answers and are flagged, never rejected.

Prompts exclude name, age, sex, civil status, religion, ethnicity and location, and treat resume text as data, not instructions. Combined score = resume 60% + questionnaire 40%, so a polished questionnaire cannot hide a weak resume (a 2 and a 100 combine to 41.2 in testing).

What testing caught

  • A vague rubric anchor scored an average candidate too low; fixed in the Sheet, no node edits
  • A questionnaire claimed experience the resume did not show; added the claim check
  • An honest "4 years" answer was flagged; the AI now labels claims and code decides what counts
  • A Config change released a pending Reject early; the release time is now locked

Guardrails confirmed every run

  • No candidate rejected by the AI
  • Knockout failures flagged for review
  • Duplicates linked, not re-scored
  • No candidate email mentions scores or AI

Limits and roadmap

What v1.0 leaves out on purpose

Known limits

  • Text-based PDF resumes only
  • One job per Sheet
  • Rescheduling is done in Google Calendar
  • Decisions are picked up every 2 minutes
  • Old data is deleted by hand

Next versions

  • v1.3: rescheduling and interview reminders
  • v2.0: several jobs at once, scanned-resume OCR, redaction before AI, retention auto-delete, a recruiter dashboard
