The problem

Growth was outrunning memory

Before

  • Requests scattered across Messenger, calls and referrals
  • Follow-ups and product requests kept in the owner's head
  • Quotations prepared by hand, with no record of who answered
  • No single view of which deals were open, won or lost

After (V1)

  • Every website request becomes a GHL contact, company and deal
  • The owner gets a Telegram alert with priority, summary and missing details
  • Buttons move the deal and write a note; GHL stays the record
  • Quotes are GHL Estimates; the customer's answer triggers an alert and sets the deal value

The owner already knows how to sell. The goal was to give him a system, not to replace his sales process with AI.

Architecture

GHL holds the records, n8n moves them, the owner decides

Every Telegram button re-reads the deal from GHL before acting. If the stage no longer matches, the tap is refused with the stage the deal is actually in.

AI lead analysis

Rules first, model second

The model receives only the request fields and returns a structured result: priority, reason, summary, missing information and uncertainty. The review checks run first, because the model otherwise stopped at the first matching rule.

🟠 REVIEWChecked first: patient or clinical details, non-medical items, an unclear request, or low confidence. Also used when the AI call fails 3 times, so no lead is ever dropped.
🔴 HIGHCustomer says it is urgent and names specific items.
🟡 MEDIUMSpecific items, with urgency Soon, Flexible or not stated.
🟢 LOWGeneral inquiry.

The AI never mentions prices, stock, delivery times, certifications or medical claims. Quotation totals come from GHL Estimates, never from the model, and the owner approves every quote.

Reliability

Failures are caught and reported

Safeguards

  • Duplicate check: a lead already marked SUCCESS is skipped
  • Retries (3×) only on safe read and update calls; create calls are not retried, to avoid duplicates
  • Automation Status on each deal: PROCESSING, SUCCESS or REQUIRES HUMAN REVIEW
  • Any live failure sends a 🚨 alert naming the failed step, with a link to the run
  • Only the owner's Telegram account can use the buttons

Deliberate business decisions

  • A deal counts as Won only at Completed, so orders still being delivered stay on the open board
  • Lost always needs one of five reasons
  • Deal value comes from the accepted quote total
  • No patient records, diagnoses or prescriptions are stored

Scope

What V1 does not do yet

Known limits in V1

  • Website not yet published on a business domain
  • Webhooks have no shared secret
  • No Telegram alert for consultation bookings
  • Stages between Contacted and Quotation Sent are moved by hand
  • Sample products and prices only; the real price list is pending
  • A run that hangs leaves the deal in PROCESSING without an alert
Planned for V2 · not built
  • Delivery tracking fields and buttons (out for delivery, partial, on hold, delivered)
  • Daily 8:00 summary of deals needing action
  • Webhook secret and a published business website
  • Alerts for consultation bookings and cancellations
