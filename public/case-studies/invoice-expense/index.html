Invoice & Expense Processing Automation

A small business receives vendor invoices by email and keys them in by hand. This n8n system reads each invoice with AI, checks every number in code, blocks duplicates, asks the owner to approve anything over $500, files the PDF and records every outcome in a Google Sheets register, usually within 7 to 16 seconds.

Client questions

What business owners ask about it

Who needs it?

Small and mid-sized businesses receiving roughly 20 to 500 vendor invoices a month and handling them by hand: agencies, clinics, e-commerce shops, property managers, consultants with contractors, and the bookkeepers or VAs who run accounts payable for them.

Why do companies need this?

Manual accounts payable leaks money and time: duplicate payments from resent invoices, wrong totals because nobody re-adds the math, spending without a recorded approval, hours of data entry, and late payments because invoices wait for the weekly catch-up. Each gap gets a specific check here, and every invoice leaves a record.

How does it work?

An invoice arrives by email or form. Its text is read, AI extracts the fields, code validates them and checks for duplicates, anything over $500 waits for a one-click approval, then the PDF is filed and a row is written to the Register. Anything that fails a check goes to Needs Review with the reason.

How does it cut the cycle from about a week to a day?

Most of the delay is waiting, not working. The example below is illustrative (a business that does accounts payable in a weekly batch), not a measured client result.

Does the AI decide what gets paid?

No. AI reads, code checks, humans approve. The AI only converts text into fields; fixed rules decide whether an invoice passes, and a person approves every invoice over the limit.

What if the AI reads something wrong?

The code catches it. Totals are re-added, the AI's invoice number and total must appear in the PDF text, and missing fields fail validation. In testing the AI returned "not found" for a missing invoice number instead of inventing one, and copied a wrong vendor total exactly as printed, which the math check flagged.

Does it pay vendors automatically?

No, by design. Payment stays in the company's bank or accounting tool, under the owner's control.

What happens when a vendor sends the same invoice twice?

It is marked Duplicate with a note naming the original record and filed for review. The match is vendor + invoice number, ignoring spacing and punctuation, and it also catches two copies arriving in the same run.

Can it read scanned or photographed invoices?

Not in V1. They are detected, sent to Needs Review with a clear note, and never sent to the AI. OCR is the first roadmap item.

What do we need to run it, and what does it cost?

A Google account (Gmail, Drive, Sheets), an n8n instance (a small VPS or n8n Cloud) and an OpenRouter key. The AI costs about $0.0004 per invoice (estimate), roughly 4 cents per 100 invoices. The team keeps using email, a spreadsheet and Drive.

Is our data safe? Where does it live?

Invoices, PDFs and the Register stay in the company's own Google account; n8n runs on a server the business controls and keeps run logs there for troubleshooting. Only the invoice text goes to the AI model. Access uses Google sign-in (OAuth), not shared passwords.

Can we change the limit, approvers or categories? Other currencies?

Yes. The approval limit, approver, currency and category list are single settings, each tested with one invoice before going live. V1 uses one currency per setup; others are flagged for review.

Does it connect to QuickBooks or Xero?

Not in V1; the Register is the hand-off point. Bill creation in QuickBooks or Xero is on the roadmap.

What happens if something breaks?

The AI, Sheets, Drive and Gmail-label steps retry 3 times. If a run still fails, a separate workflow emails the failed step, the error, a plain-English likely fix and a link to the run. The original email or form submission remains, so nothing is lost.

What does our team still do, and how long is setup?

Approve invoices over the limit (one click), clear the weekly Needs Review list, and pay vendors as usual; the Owner SOP covers each task. This V1 was built and tested in 7 days; adapting it to a new business is estimated at 1 to 2 weeks.

Scope

What V1 does not do yet

Known limits in V1

  • Scanned or photographed invoices go to manual entry
  • Google sign-in renews weekly (app in testing mode)
  • One invoice email per check (up to ~60 an hour)
  • Settings live inside the workflow
  • No accounting-system sync
Planned for V2 · not built
  • OCR before the AI step
  • Published Google app (no weekly sign-in)
  • Approvals in a sub-workflow for batches
  • A Config tab in the Register
  • QuickBooks / Xero bill creation
